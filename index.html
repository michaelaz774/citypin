Why not just call 311?

Complement, not replacement
311 reports a defect where you are standing, now.
It cannot carry an opinion about a place you are afraid to walk to, or about a bike lane that doesn't exist yet. A comment form strips out the exact spot, the sightline and the scale. CityPin keeps all three: a pin carries coordinates, a category and the view you had when you left it, and it exports straight into the GIS a city already runs — or into the 311 queue as a georeferenced request.

Built for SDG 11.

UN Sustainable Development Goal 11
11.3 · Inclusive, participatory urbanization
Consultation stops selecting for who is free on a Tuesday evening. Older people, disabled people, shift workers and parents report from home, standing in the spot.
11.2 · Accessible transport for all
Pins mark the barriers on the way to transit: missing curb cuts, blocked sidewalks, no seating at the stop.
11.7 · Safe, inclusive public space
Dark underpasses, unshaded waits and unusable parks get a location, a category and a count.

For planners.

GeoJSON · CSV
A ranked map, not a comment pile
Press P for the planner panel: every pin with category, note, votes, distance and age. Filter and sort, then click a row to teleport to it.
Opens in the GIS you already run
Export a GeoJSON FeatureCollection (longitude, latitude) or a CSV. No procurement, no integration project.
Limitations: building heights outside the core are OSM defaults; pins are unauthenticated; moderation is not built.

Open by default.

OpenStreetMap · CC0 characters
City data comes from OpenStreetMap and the engine is open source, so any city with OSM buildings can be stood up by re-running the fetch script against a new bounding box.
