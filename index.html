Report a street problem

CityPin lets you walk downtown Toronto in 3D and leave a note standing exactly where the street fails — no meeting, no form. Pins sync live to everyone and planners can export them straight into their GIS.

How it works
  1. Walk downtown Toronto in first person, straight in the browser.
  2. Aim the crosshair at the spot that is failing.
  3. Press P, pick a category, and type up to 48 characters.
  4. Everyone walking the city sees your pin instantly.
  5. Walk past someone else's pin and press U to agree with it.
  6. Planners open L and export the ranked list as GeoJSON or CSV.
